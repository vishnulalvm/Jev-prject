      How it works
      
      
      Jev is a typed decision model for NSE and BSE stocks. It turns the readings you enter into a fixed set of answers, so the same inputs always give the same call.
      
      
      
        
      1
      You enter what you know
      Only the symbol and price are required. Every blank field is treated as neutral, and Jev lowers its confidence when data is thin.
      
        
      2
      Jev answers 20 typed questions
      Valuation, balance sheet, momentum and relative strength, macro and risk, and upside and catalysts. Each answer is a Choice or a Score, with a Noul that says why.
      
        
      3
      One call, with its odds
      All 20 questions go to the Jev model in one request, evaluated in parallel. Jev's probabilities set the call and confidence; code adds the trade plan, audit and guardrails.
      
        
      4
      Ask follow-ups
      Ask Jev any yes/no question and it returns a Noul, the probability of yes, judged against the same state. On claude.ai, Claude can also explain the analysis in plain language.
      
      
      
      
      
      
      Where the data comes from
      
      
      Jev does not fetch live market data yet. You enter every value, so it helps to pull them from the same few places each time.
      
      
      
        
      Your chartKite, Upstox or TradingView, daily candles
      Price, EMA 20/50/200, RSI(14), MACD, ATR(14), volume vs 20-day average, support and resistance, 3-month returns for the stock and the Nifty.
      
        
      nseindia.comUpdated daily
      India VIX, FII/DII provisional cash data, the ASM and GSM surveillance lists, results dates from corporate announcements, and average traded value on the quote page.
      
        
      Screener.inOr the company's filings
      P/E and sector P/E, PEG, free cash flow, debt/equity, interest cover and promoter pledge. For banks and NBFCs: P/B, gross NPA, capital adequacy and ROA.
      
        
      Your judgementDefine it the same way each time
      Nifty and sector trend (for example, above a rising 50 DMA is bullish), market sentiment, next catalyst, and your notes.
      
      
      
      
      Limits to know
      
      
      
        
      Not backtested
      The weights are expert rules, not fitted to historical results. Treat the probability as a way to compare setups, not as true odds.
      
        
      No order flow
      Jev doesn't see options open interest, delivery percentage, block deals or intraday tape. Add anything important as a note for Ask Jev.
      
        
      Short selling
      In the Indian cash market, delivery-based shorting isn't allowed. A Sell call means exit or avoid; shorting needs F&O or intraday.
      
      
      
      
      Jev produces probability estimates from the inputs you provide. It is not SEBI-registered investment advice.
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      Why Jev?
      
      
      A fast, reliable and low-cost decision model for Indian equities.
      
      
      
        
      Fast
      All 20 questions go to Jev in one request and are answered in parallel.
      
        
      Typed decisions
      Always Buy, Hold or Sell, with Scores from 1 to 5. Never vague.
      
        
      Consistent
      Same inputs, same answer, every time. No drift between runs.
      
        
      Probabilistic
      Every call carries a probability and a confidence level.
      
        
      Low cost
      One API call per analysis. No terminal subscription.
